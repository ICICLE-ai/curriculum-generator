   https://scikit-learn.org/stable/documentation.html  
   *Official docs covering classifiers, metrics, and model evaluation.*

2. **NumPy (Arrays & flattening images)**
   https://numpy.org/doc/stable/user/quickstart.html

3. **Pandas (Optional for tabular data)**
