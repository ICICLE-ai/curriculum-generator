# Digital Agriculture Curriculum

Grade: 9
Duration: 14 weeks


## Introduction to Digital Agriculture

Overview of digital farming, sensors, and AI.

Project: Soil health monitoring

### Activities

- Discuss the importance of Introduction to Digital Agriculture.

- Hands-on activity related to Introduction to Digital Agriculture.

- Reflection and Q&A session.



## Crop Disease Detection

AI models to detect crop diseases from images.

Project: Soybean leaf disease classifier

### Activities

- Discuss the importance of Crop Disease Detection.

- Hands-on activity related to Crop Disease Detection.

- Reflection and Q&A session.



## Recommendation Engines

Create simple recommendation systems for fertilizers.

Project: Fertilizer recommendation app

### Activities

- Discuss the importance of Recommendation Engines.

- Hands-on activity related to Recommendation Engines.

- Reflection and Q&A session.
